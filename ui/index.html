Central risk book

The desk covers risk it considers excessive by itself: anything above the trigger is not risk you chose, it is risk that got away from you. Below the trigger nothing is hedged, because that is the risk you are paid to take. Legs are ordinary orders, so caps, the kill switch and the daily stop still apply, and they only leave without you when AUTO is armed and auto_hedge is in auto mode.

The rest is yours. For each event: what it loses in its worst state, the protection on offer, and what each one costs per dollar of worst case it removes. The desk marks the ones under your price limit and calls what is left after them the optimal remainder - the risk worth keeping, because covering it costs more than it protects. Reduce-position preview

Selling part of the held outcome reduces its directional exposure. Buying its exact opposite has the same payoff effect. This calculator sends no orders.

Quotes may be stale or include your own orders. A buyer and sufficient depth are required. Cancel remaining entry orders before closing so they cannot refill the position. No automatic stops or hedges are active.

When does closing make sense?

There is no minimum holding time that earns profit. Compare the executable exit price with your entry and fees, your current view of the outcome, and your maximum acceptable loss. A 72¢ YES quote is not a 72¢ NO exit. A take-profit is a sale target; a stop-loss is an attempted exit after an adverse move, not a guaranteed fill. If trading has closed, wait for settlement.

Weather source validation

NWS forecasts continue as research. Weather proposals whose rules name The Weather Company or lack a supported NWS source cannot be approved or auto-submitted. A matching source alone does not establish model profitability. Manual tickets are discretionary and still go through all entry risk limits.
